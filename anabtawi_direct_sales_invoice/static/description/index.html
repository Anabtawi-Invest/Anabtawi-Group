Anabtawi Direct Sales Invoice

Warehouse-approved direct customer invoicing with standard Odoo 19 inventory, accounting, payment terms, and end-to-end traceability.

Controlled from request to release

Draft → Warehouse Approval → Preparation → Pickup Release → Standard Invoice → Completion

Customer pricing

Loads the assigned standard customer pricelist, currency, payment terms, fiscal position, quantity rules, and date-valid pricing. Manual prices require permission, a reason, and optional manager approval.

Warehouse control

Scopes availability to enabled and permitted warehouses. Supports full, partial, rejected, direct-delivery, two-step dispatch, and multi-warehouse fulfillment.

Standard stock

Creates ordinary pickings and moves, respects reservations, locations, lots/serials, removal strategies, backorders, and standard return pickings.

Standard accounting

Creates ordinary customer invoices with standard taxes, payment terms, posting, payments, reconciliation, residuals, and credit notes. Internal pricelist data is not added to the customer PDF.

Auditable and secure

Immutable approval events, chatter activities, company and warehouse record rules, role-based workflow actions, RPC write protection, idempotent stock/invoice creation, and internal operational reports are included.
