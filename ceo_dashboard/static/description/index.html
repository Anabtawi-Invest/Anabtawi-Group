6 Operational Domains

Everything You Need In One Dashboard

Organized into clean, focused tabs with dedicated KPIs, visual vector gauges, and interactive drilldowns.

📊

Executive Summary

The 30-Second Morning Pulse
  • 5 Headline Metrics: Total Revenue, Net Profit, Live Cash, Critical Overdue Invoices, and MTD Orders.
  • Revenue vs Target Gauge: Semicircular vector gauge showing monthly quota pace & attainment percentage.
  • Working Capital Ledger: Accounts Receivable vs Accounts Payable, plus DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) indicator.
  • Operations & HR Pulse: Low stock alert count, pending deliveries, time-off requests, and over-budget projects.
💰

Finance & Invoicing

Liquidity, Margins & Collections
  • Revenue vs LY: Compare current period revenue & expenses against same period last year.
  • Gross & Net Profitability: Live Gross Margin % and Net Margin retention rates.
  • Cash & Bank Breakdown: Multi-account balance view across operating, treasury, and payment gateways.
  • Top 5 Overdue Customers: Direct dunning action buttons and aging breakdown (30/60/90+ days).
🎯

Sales & CRM Pipeline

Quota Attainment & Deal Flow
  • Sales Orders Velocity: Confirmed sales orders broken down by Today, This Week, and This Month.
  • Pipeline Health: Total open opportunities, Win Rate %, Average Deal Size, and new weekly leads.
  • Top 5 Customers by Revenue: Track primary revenue drivers and recurring account volume.
  • Closing This Month: Weighted forecast cards with win probabilities and pipeline stage badges.
📦

Inventory & Operations

Stock Valuation & Fulfillment
  • Inventory Valuation: Total capital tied up in warehouse stock and turnover velocity.
  • Low Stock Reorder Alerts: Products below safety stock with threshold alerts.
  • Direct RFQ Generation: Trigger vendor Request for Quotations directly from the dashboard table.
  • Shipment Monitor: Pending customer outgoing deliveries and incoming receipts due today.
👥

HR & Attendance

Workforce, Leaves & Hiring
  • Headcount & Capacity: Active employee headcount, staff on leave today, and late clock-in logs.
  • In-Dashboard Leave Approval: Click [Approve] or [Refuse] directly without opening leaves menu!
  • Out of Office Tracker: See who is away with expected return dates for team planning.
  • Talent Recruitment Pipeline: Open job vacancies with applicant counts and hiring stages.
📁

Project & Helpdesk

Milestones, Budgets & SLA
  • Deadline Monitor: Overdue project tasks and tasks scheduled for completion today.
  • Budget Health: Active projects exceeding allocated hours and budget thresholds.
  • Billable Utilization: Recorded billable hours compared against company targets.
  • Urgent SLA Support: Live counter of high-priority helpdesk tickets awaiting resolution.
